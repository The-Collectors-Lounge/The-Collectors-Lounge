CITY32 · MÉRIDA

The right address
for the right audience.

City32 places The Collectors Lounge inside one of Mérida's most distinctive mixed-use environments — where retail, gastronomy, lifestyle and contemporary architecture come together in a distinctly high-end setting.

For a Maison entering Mexico, it creates a natural setting for welcoming high-net-worth clients, collectors and international guests. A five-star hotel, a curated food hall and first-rate gastronomic restaurants create a sophisticated hospitality ecosystem, while the architecture and overall environment reinforce the sense of occasion around every meeting, private appointment or event.

City32 gives Maisons a permanent address without the burden of a permanent boutique — with the infrastructure, atmosphere and audience to host, impress and build lasting relationships.
